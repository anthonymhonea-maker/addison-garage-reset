How a clean-out works

One visit, one flat price, and a garage you can park in when we leave. Here is what the day looks like from the first text to the last sweep.

The day, start to finish

  1. Send photos, get a price

    Text or chat us two or three photos of the garage. We reply with a flat price, usually the same day. That price holds unless the garage looks very different from the photos.

  2. Everything comes out

    We carry the whole garage onto the driveway and lay it out on tarps by type: tools, sports gear, holiday boxes, paint, and so on. Seeing it all at once is what makes the next step fast.

  3. You decide: keep, donate, or trash

    We walk the driveway with you and you point. Most people finish in under half an hour. Nothing leaves without your say, and anything you're unsure about stays.

  4. We sweep and put the keepers back

    The empty garage gets swept wall to wall. Then what you're keeping goes back in zones, with the things you use every week closest to the door and the once-a-year boxes up high or in the back.

  5. The rest leaves with us

    Donations go to a local donation center and trash goes to the transfer station. One full truckload is included in every job. You pull the car in.

What we haul, and what we can't

We take

  • Furniture, mattresses, and old shelving
  • Boxes, bags, and general household junk
  • Broken tools, bikes, and sports gear
  • Scrap metal and lumber
  • Working items you'd like donated

We can't take

  • Paint, solvents, and pool chemicals
  • Motor oil, gasoline, and propane tanks
  • Tires and car batteries

Disposal sites won't accept these from a hauler. We set them aside in one spot and tell you where residents can drop them off.

Common questions

Do I need to be home?

For the sorting step, yes. Plan on being around for about half an hour once everything is on the driveway. You're free the rest of the time.

What if it rains?

We reschedule at no charge. Your things spend part of the day on the driveway, so we only work on dry days.

Where do you work?

Addison and the neighborhoods right around it. If you're a little farther out, ask in the chat and we'll tell you if we can make it.

How do I pay?

When the job is done and you've seen the garage. No deposit.
